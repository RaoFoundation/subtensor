A **derivative position** lets you bet on the price of a subnet's alpha
without holding it. A **short** profits when alpha falls. A **long** profits
when alpha rises. You put down a **cushion** (your deposit) and the chain
borrows a matching slice of the subnet pool for you. When you close, the
borrowed slice goes back, the pool is paid a small **borrow fee**, and you
get your cushion plus or minus the price move.

There are no synthetic tokens. Nothing is minted or burned. Every position is
built from the pool's own TAO and alpha, and every position has a hard
**expiry** of 30 days.

Shipped in the [v454 release](/releases/v454-upgrade). This guide uses the
same illustrative pool as the staking guides — **10,000 τ / 200,000 α at
0.05 τ/α** — with round numbers. Read live values with
[`derivatives-params`](/docs/query/derivatives-params) and
[`derivative-positions`](/docs/query/derivative-positions) before acting.

## What a position is

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Cushion** | Your deposit, in TAO from your coldkey balance. It is held by the pallet while the position is open and returned at close, minus the fee and any loss. |
| **Exposure** | The TAO value the position moves with. At 1x leverage it equals your cushion. |
| **Borrowed slice** | The fraction `phi` of the pool the position lifts out: `phi × TAO reserve` and `phi × alpha reserve`. |
| **Proceeds** | What the opening trade produced. A short sells the lifted alpha, so its proceeds are TAO. A long buys alpha with the lifted TAO, so its proceeds are alpha. |
| **Debt** | What must go back to the pool at close: the lifted alpha (short) or the lifted TAO (long). |
| **Escrow** | The other half of the lifted slice. It sits untouched in the pallet and returns to the pool at close. |
| **Borrow fee** | A per-day amount fixed at open and paid to the pool at close, one-day minimum. A short pays `5 τ × phi` per day (the share of the pool it lifted); a long pays `0.02%` of exposure per day. |
| **Expiry** | Open block + 216,000 blocks (~30 days). After that, anyone may close the position for you. |

One position per coldkey, per subnet, per side. You may hold a long and a
short on the same subnet at once; they are independent.

## How open and close work

The pallet never trades against you and never holds a book. It lifts a slice
of the pool, trades that slice through the same balancer swap you use when
you stake, and reverses the trade at close.

The deck below walks one position through all eight steps. Press play, or
step through with the arrows. Flip **side** to see a long instead of a short,
and **what happens next** to see the losing path as well as the winning one.

<DerivativesLifecycle />

Three details behind the slides:

- **Lifting is price-neutral.** The pallet removes the same fraction `phi`
  from both reserves ([`lift_liquidity`](/code/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/derivatives_pool.rs)).
  The price is a ratio, so it does not move. The pool is briefly shallower,
  which is why exposure is capped.
- **The open trade is a real swap.** A short pushes the price down at open; a
  long pushes it up. You pay slippage on the way in and on the way out,
  exactly like a spot trade of the same size. Because the pallet sells alpha
  it just lifted, rather than alpha it bought, there is no round trip that
  can be arbitraged.
- **Returning is price-neutral too.** At close the pool gets back its slice
  plus the fee through
  [`return_liquidity`](/code/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/derivatives_pool.rs),
  which adds an uneven TAO/alpha pair without moving the price. Any profit
  you take leaves the pool a little lighter on one side; any loss you pay
  leaves it heavier.

The borrowed slice is fixed at open:

$$
\phi = \frac{L \cdot \text{cushion}}{\text{TAO reserve}}
$$

where $L$ is `leverage_percent / 100` (1.0 on mainnet). A 100 τ cushion on a
10,000 τ pool lifts 1% of the pool: 100 τ and 2,000 α. See
[`pool_fraction`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/position.rs).

## What you get back

<DerivativesPayoff />

Three things to read off the picture:

- **The line crosses your cushion at the open price.** Close with no price
  move and you get your 100 τ back, minus the fee and a little slippage.
- **1x means one-for-one.** Alpha down 20% pays a short about 20 τ on a
  100 τ cushion. Alpha up 20% pays a long the same.
- **The line stops at zero.** If the closing trade cannot repay what the
  position borrowed, the position is underwater: you are paid nothing,
  whatever the pallet still holds goes to the pool, and the pool carries the
  remaining shortfall
  ([`do_settle`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/settle.rs)). For a
  short that is near a doubling; for a long it is alpha going to zero. The
  10% pool cap keeps that gap small relative to the pool. At 1x a long with a
  TAO cushion can never be underwater: the cushion equals the debt.

Cushions are TAO only. That keeps the payoff the plain line above, and it
means a subnet team cannot post alpha it minted to itself as collateral.

The fee is the only cost besides slippage. It is fixed per day when you open,
from the parameters in force then, and charged at close:

$$
\text{fee} = \text{fee per day} \times \max(1, \text{days open})
$$

The two sides price differently because they carry different risks for the
pool:

- **Short:** `fee per day = short_fee_per_day × phi`, with `phi` the share of
  the pool you lifted. Borrowing 1% of *any* pool costs 0.05 τ a day
  (5 τ × 1%), so 1.5 τ over 30 days. The pool's risk on a short is a pump,
  and in a constant-product pool the cost of a pump scales with `1 / TAO
  reserve`; a fixed TAO amount per unit of pool share tracks that.
- **Long:** `fee per day = long_rate_per_day × exposure`. A 100 τ exposure
  costs 0.02 τ a day, so 0.6 τ over 30 days. The pool's risk on a long is a
  crash, and that risk does not depend on pool size.

In the illustrative 10,000 τ pool a 100 τ short and a 100 τ long both lift
`phi = 1%`; the short pays 1.5 τ a month, the long 0.6 τ. Changing the
parameters later does not reprice a running position. See
[`accrued_fee`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/position.rs) and
[`DerivativesParams::fee_per_day`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/position.rs).

<Callout type="note">
`btcli deriv list` shows an **estimated** close value: cushion + proceeds −
debt − fee, everything at spot. The real settlement pays slippage on the
buyback or sale, so expect slightly less.
</Callout>

## Open a position

Replace netuid `7` with your target subnet. Both sides take the same options:
`--amount` is the cushion, in TAO, taken from your coldkey balance.

```bash
# Check the pallet's live parameters first
btcli deriv params --json

# Short subnet 7 with a 100 τ cushion (dry-run, then execute)
btcli deriv short --netuid 7 --amount 100 --dry-run -w my_coldkey
btcli deriv short --netuid 7 --amount 100 -w my_coldkey

# Long subnet 7 with a 100 τ cushion
btcli deriv long --netuid 7 --amount 100 -w my_coldkey
```

The same in Python:

```python
await client.execute(bt.OpenShort(netuid=7, amount=100), wallet)
await client.execute(bt.OpenLong(netuid=7, amount=100), wallet)
```

The call fails if:

| Error | Why |
|-------|-----|
| `SideDisabled` | Root has switched that side off with `sudo_set_params`. |
| `SubnetNotDynamic` | The subnet has no pool (root, or a static subnet). |
| `PositionExists` | You already hold that side on that subnet. Close it first. |
| `DepositTooLow` | The cushion is less than `min_deposit_tao` (0.1 τ). |
| `ExposureTooLarge` | `phi` would be 100% or more of the pool. |
| `PoolCapExceeded` | All open positions of that side already borrow `max_pool_share` (10%) of the lent reserve. Try a smaller cushion or wait for others to close. |

## Watch and close

```bash
# Your open positions, with fee so far, blocks to expiry, and estimated value
btcli deriv list -w my_coldkey
btcli deriv list --coldkey <ss58> --netuid 7 --json

# Close your short on subnet 7
btcli deriv close --netuid 7 --side short -w my_coldkey

# Close someone else's expired position (permissionless once expired)
btcli deriv close --netuid 7 --side long --owner <their-ss58> -w my_coldkey
```

```python
positions = await client.prices.derivative_positions(coldkey_ss58="5C...")
one = await client.prices.derivative_position(coldkey_ss58="5C...", netuid=7, side="Short")
await client.execute(bt.ClosePosition(netuid=7, side="Short"), wallet)
```

Closing is atomic: [`do_settle`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/settle.rs)
reverses the open trade, repays the pool, collects the fee, pays you, and
returns the slice — or the whole call reverts. You may close at any block.
Closing on the same day you opened still pays one day of fee.

## Roll: stay in past expiry

A position cannot be extended, but it can be **rolled**: settled at today's
price and reopened in the same transaction
([`do_roll`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/settle.rs)).

```bash
# Close the short on subnet 7 and reopen it with whatever came back
btcli deriv roll --netuid 7 --side short -w my_coldkey

# Same, adding 50 τ to the new cushion
btcli deriv roll --netuid 7 --side short --add 50 -w my_coldkey
```

```python
await client.execute(bt.RollPosition(netuid=7, side="Short", top_up=50), wallet)
```

What happens, in order:

1. The old position is settled exactly like `close`. The pool gets its slice
   and the fee so far; your loss or profit is realized now.
2. What reached you (cushion plus profit, minus loss and fee), plus any
   `--add`, becomes the cushion of a new position on the same side. It gets
   today's entry price and a full `lifetime_blocks`.

A roll is a real settlement, so the pool's risk is bounded the same way as
with a close: no position ever lives longer than `lifetime_blocks` without
being marked to market. If what comes back is below `min_deposit_tao`, or the
pool cap is reached, the roll fails and the old position stays as it was;
`close` instead. Only the owner may roll.

## Expiry

A position may stay open for `lifetime_blocks` (216,000 blocks, about 30
days). After that:

- The chain sweeps expired positions in
  [`on_idle`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/lib.rs) via
  [`sweep_expired`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/lib.rs), up to 32 per
  block, and settles them exactly as a normal close.
- If a sweep fails (for example, the pool cannot fill the buyback at that
  moment), the chain emits `SettleFailed` and tries again 300 blocks later,
  up to three times. After that the position stays open and waits for a
  manual close. `SettleFailed.retry_at` tells you which case you are in.
- Anyone may call `close` with `--owner` on an expired position. Before
  expiry that call fails with `NotExpired`.

The exact expiry block may be a few blocks later than open + 216,000 when
that block's expiry queue is already full; the position's `expires_at` is
the number to trust.

## Subnet dissolution

If a subnet is dissolved while positions are open, settling them is the
**first** cleanup phase
([`DerivativesSettle`](/code/pallets/subtensor/src/subnets/dissolution.rs)).
Positions are **unwound**, not settled: the lifted slice goes back to the pool
in kind, your TAO cushion comes back, and no fee is charged
([`unwind`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/settle.rs)). Any profit or
loss at that moment is cancelled. The pool then proceeds through the normal
dissolution payout with its full reserves.

## Parameters

Root-set, read with [`derivatives-params`](/docs/query/derivatives-params).
Mainnet defaults from
[`DerivativesParams::defaults`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/position.rs).
Root sets them with `sudo_set_params`, which rejects a zero
`leverage_percent`, `max_pool_share`, or `lifetime_blocks`; use the enable
flags to pause a side.

| Parameter | Default | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| `shorts_enabled` | `true` | Shorts may be opened. Existing shorts can always be closed. |
| `longs_enabled` | `true` | Longs may be opened. |
| `leverage_percent` | `100` | Exposure as a share of the cushion. 100 = 1x. |
| `max_pool_share` | `10%` | Largest share of the lent reserve all open positions of one side on one subnet may borrow together. |
| `lifetime_blocks` | `216,000` | Maximum time a position may stay open (~30 days). |
| `short_fee_per_day` | `5 τ` | What a short pays per day for borrowing the whole pool; a position pays this times its `phi`. Fixed at open, paid at close, one-day minimum. |
| `long_rate_per_day` | `0.02%` | Fee per day on a long's TAO exposure. Fixed at open, paid at close, one-day minimum. |
| `min_deposit_tao` | `0.1 τ` | Smallest cushion. |

## Rules worth remembering

- One position per coldkey, subnet, and side. A long and a short on the same
  subnet are allowed and independent.
- Cushions are TAO only. Alpha you hold stays staked and keeps earning.
- You can close at any block; you cannot extend past 30 days. `roll` to stay
  in: it settles at today's price and reopens in one transaction.
- Slippage is real on both legs. Size positions against the pool's depth,
  not against your balance.
- Profit comes from the pool; loss goes to the pool. The 1x leverage and 10%
  cap bound how much either can move the pool.

For the pool mechanics underneath, see [Staking pools](/docs/concepts/staking-pools).
