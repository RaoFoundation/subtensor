---
title: Gamma
description: Dollar-valued service credits minted by burning a subnet's alpha, held in deposits keyed by API-key hotkeys and spent by each hotkey at the subnet's services.
---

Gamma is a credit a subnet can accept as payment for its service, valued in US
dollars. Once gamma is enabled network-wide and on a subnet, a coldkey mints
that subnet's gamma by burning its alpha, bought with TAO or taken from stake.
One gamma is minted for each dollar the burned alpha is worth. Other subnets
can also buy it with [shares of their emission](/docs/concepts/emission-weights#gamma-shares).

Gamma is held in **deposits**. Each deposit belongs to a coldkey and is keyed by
a hotkey, which [signs](/docs/guides/signed-requests) the customer's requests to the service, so the hotkey
works as an API key. The hotkey spends the deposit at the service, which credits
it with what it spent.

The API-key hotkey can be unassociated. If it is already associated with a
coldkey, only that coldkey can open a deposit at it, including by moving gamma
into a new deposit. This prevents someone else from reserving an owned hotkey's
deposit slot, including a subnet owner's hotkey used to receive gamma emission.
The funding coldkey controls the deposit's daily cap and balance. The key
holder signs service requests and spends the gamma on chain. Moving the balance
to another API key revokes access to those credits through the old key.

There is only one deposit per `(netuid, hotkey)`. If another coldkey has already
opened that slot, use another API key; staking ownership does not grant control
over the existing deposit.

A coldkey can also hold gamma on no hotkey, as its **balance** on a subnet, and
send it to other coldkeys (see [Balances and transfers](#balances-and-transfers)).
Gamma cannot be converted back into alpha or TAO. Amounts are in gamma, with
10^9 units per gamma.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Coldkey | mint into its deposits or its balance, move gamma between them and its balance, send gamma to other coldkeys, set each deposit's daily spend cap, close empty deposits |
| Hotkey | spend its deposit at the subnet's services, without a fee; sign requests to them |
| Validator or subnet owner hotkey | publish a [service](/docs/guides/services) on its subnet, signed by its coldkey |
| Subnet owner | enable gamma on its subnet with [`gamma_enabled`](/docs/hyperparameters/gamma-enabled); pay shares of its subnet's emission into other subnets' gamma |
| Lease beneficiary | enable gamma and publish the subnet owner hotkey's service through its `SubnetLeaseBeneficiary` proxy |
| Root | enable gamma network-wide and on any subnet but root, whitelist subnets for gamma emission, and set the TAO/USD price oracle |
| Oracle | feed the TAO/USD price |

## Minting

Minting needs gamma enabled on the subnet and a fresh
[TAO/USD price](#the-taousd-price). Mint with TAO into a deposit at a fresh
hotkey:

```bash
btcli wallet new-hotkey -H API_KEY
btcli gamma mint --netuid 1 --hotkey API_KEY --amount-tao 10
```

```python
quote = await client.gamma.quote_mint_gamma(netuid=1, amount_tao=10)
await client.execute(bt.MintGamma(netuid=1, hotkey_ss58=api_key, amount_tao=10), wallet)
```

[`mint-gamma`](/docs/tx/mint-gamma) buys alpha with the TAO and burns it, with
the same pool and flow accounting as [`stake-burn`](/docs/tx/stake-burn), and
creates no stake position. With no hotkey, a mint credits your
[balance](#balances-and-transfers) on the subnet instead, to deposit or send
later.
[`mint-gamma-with-stake`](/docs/tx/mint-gamma-with-stake) burns stake you
already hold instead:

```bash
btcli gamma mint-with-stake --netuid 1 --hotkey API_KEY --origin-hotkey VALIDATOR --amount-alpha 100
```

`--amount-alpha all` burns the whole stake.

The burned alpha is valued at the lower of two prices:

```
TAO value = alpha × min(gamma price, spot price)
gamma     = TAO value × TAO/USD price
```

Every subnet but root keeps a gamma price, whether or not gamma is enabled.
Registration sets it to the median subnet alpha price, which the new pool is
created at; a subnet registered before gamma starts at its spot price. Each
block it falls to the spot price at once and rises toward it by ln 2 / 600 of
the gap, so the gap halves every 600 blocks, about 2 hours. A rise is at most ln
2 / 600 of the gamma price itself, so however far spot jumps, the gamma price at
most doubles every 600 blocks. The spot price is read before the mint trades.
Alpha is valued at these prices, not at what selling it would return, so a burn
that is large for the pool is worth more than its sale.

The SDK quotes the mint first and fails it if the minted gamma would fall more
than `rate_tolerance` (5% by default) below the quote. The with-stake quote
assumes the whole amount is burned; the SDK quotes no more than the stake you
hold. Once the mint runs, `result.data["minted"]` holds the deposit's
`hotkey_ss58` (`None` for your balance) and `daily_spend_cap_gamma`, and the
`alpha_burned`, `tao_value` and `gamma` minted, as exact decimal strings.

A mint fails with [`GammaDisabled`](/docs/errors/chain/GammaDisabled) while
gamma is off network-wide or on the subnet. Turning one off stops new mints,
and gamma emission to the subnet waits until it is on again. Existing deposits
can still be spent, moved and closed. A mint also fails with
[`AmountTooLow`](/docs/errors/chain/AmountTooLow) below 0.1 gamma, with
[`SubtokenDisabled`](/docs/errors/chain/SubtokenDisabled) on a subnet whose
subtoken is not enabled, and with
[`SlippageTooHigh`](/docs/errors/chain/SlippageTooHigh) below its minimum. A TAO
mint above the add-stake swap input limit fails with
[`InsufficientLiquidity`](/docs/errors/chain/InsufficientLiquidity). Minting,
moving or depositing into a hotkey whose deposit belongs to another coldkey
fails with [`NonAssociatedColdKey`](/docs/errors/chain/NonAssociatedColdKey).

Through a proxy, minting, moving, depositing, withdrawing, sending and setting a
deposit's daily spend cap need an `Any` or `NonCritical` proxy. Closing a
deposit also takes a `NonTransfer` proxy.

## Deposits and API keys

Any account can be a deposit's hotkey, and it needs no registration or funds. A
hotkey holds at most one deposit per subnet, owned by the coldkey that opened
it, so create a fresh hotkey for each deposit. Requests signed with it are paid
from the deposit, so never use a staking hotkey or a coldkey. A coldkey holds at
most 64 deposits per subnet.

```bash
btcli gamma list
btcli gamma show --netuid 1 --hotkey API_KEY
```

To let someone spend your gamma, open a deposit with their hotkey. The deposit stays
yours: move the gamma back at any time.

```bash
btcli gamma move --netuid 1 --origin-hotkey THEIR_KEY --destination-hotkey API_KEY --amount-gamma all
```

[`move-gamma`](/docs/tx/move-gamma) to a hotkey with no deposit opens one with
the origin's daily spend cap. A move takes the whole balance, or at least 0.1
gamma leaving at least as much, and one that empties the origin closes it. A
deposit spent to zero stays open with its cap, so it can be refilled,
until it is closed with [`close-gamma-deposit`](/docs/tx/close-gamma-deposit):

```bash
btcli gamma close --netuid 1 --hotkey API_KEY
```

## Balances and transfers

A coldkey's **balance** on a subnet is gamma it holds on no hotkey. No key can
spend it until it is moved to a deposit, but it can be sent to other coldkeys.
[`withdraw-gamma`](/docs/tx/withdraw-gamma) moves up to an amount from a
deposit to the balance, and a withdrawal that empties the deposit closes it.
[`deposit-gamma`](/docs/tx/deposit-gamma) moves up to an amount from the
balance to a deposit. A hotkey with no deposit opens one with no daily spend
cap.

```bash
btcli gamma withdraw --netuid 1 --hotkey API_KEY --amount-gamma 5
btcli gamma deposit --netuid 1 --hotkey OTHER_API_KEY --all
btcli gamma list
```

[`transfer-gamma`](/docs/tx/transfer-gamma) sends an exact amount to another
coldkey. It comes from the signer's balance, or from its deposit at
`--origin-hotkey`, and goes to the deposit the receiver already holds at
`--destination-hotkey`, which that hotkey can spend at once and which keeps its
daily spend cap. Without it, the gamma goes to the receiver's balance, which no
key can spend until the receiver's coldkey deposits it, so name a deposit when
the receiver has one. A transfer never opens a deposit, so a receiver who wants top-ups at a key
opens one there first, with `deposit` or `mint`. Sending to a hotkey's address
puts the gamma in a balance only that address's key can move.

```bash
btcli gamma transfer --netuid 1 --dest-coldkey THEIR_COLDKEY --origin-hotkey API_KEY \
  --destination-hotkey THEIR_API_KEY --amount-gamma 5
btcli gamma transfer --netuid 1 --dest-coldkey THEIR_COLDKEY --amount-gamma 5
```

```python
await client.execute(bt.WithdrawGamma(netuid=1, hotkey_ss58=api_key, amount_gamma=5), wallet)
await client.execute(
    bt.TransferGamma(netuid=1, dest_coldkey_ss58=their_coldkey, amount_gamma=5), wallet
)
balances = await client.gamma.gamma_balances(coldkey_ss58=wallet.coldkeypub.ss58_address)
```

An amount is the whole source, or at least 0.1 gamma leaving at least as much.
A transfer takes exactly the amount given, at least 0.1 gamma, so it has no
`--all`. These calls
work while gamma is off. A transfer is not a payment to a service: only a
hotkey's spend is.

## Daily spend caps

A daily spend cap, set with
[`set-gamma-deposit-daily-spend-cap`](/docs/tx/set-gamma-deposit-daily-spend-cap),
bounds what the hotkey can spend. What it allows refills linearly at the cap
per 7200 blocks, up to the cap. A cap set where there was none starts full, and
a changed cap keeps what it allows now, up to the new cap, so ending a pause
refills from nothing. A cap of zero pauses the deposit, any other cap is at
least 0.1 gamma, and `--remove-cap` removes the cap.

While a deposit has a cap, its hotkey spends at most the highest cap plus its
refill over any period. A deposit opened with a cap starts full.

```bash
btcli gamma set-cap --netuid 1 --hotkey API_KEY --daily-spend-cap-gamma 20
```

A mint can name a new deposit's cap with `--daily-spend-cap-gamma 20`, so the
deposit never opens without it. A deposit that already has another cap fails
the mint with
[`GammaDepositDailySpendCapMismatch`](/docs/errors/chain/GammaDepositDailySpendCapMismatch),
and so does a mint into your balance, which has no cap.

### Deposits that gamma emission opens

[Gamma emission](/docs/concepts/emission-weights#gamma-shares) credits a subnet
owner's deposit at the subnet owner hotkey on the target subnet. From runtime
495, a deposit that gamma emission opens has **no cap**: the owner hotkey can
spend the gamma at once. Before runtime 495 such a deposit opened with a cap of
0, and the owner coldkey had to set a cap first.

An existing deposit keeps its cap. A deposit that is paused today stays paused
until its coldkey sets or removes the cap. No migration changes it.

Only this case changes. A deposit that you open with a mint has the cap that the
mint names, or no cap when the mint names none, as before.

```bash
# The owner coldkey limits the owner hotkey to 20 gamma a day, pauses it, or removes the cap.
btcli gamma set-cap --netuid 3 --hotkey OWNER_HOTKEY --daily-spend-cap-gamma 20
btcli gamma set-cap --netuid 3 --hotkey OWNER_HOTKEY --daily-spend-cap-gamma 0
btcli gamma set-cap --netuid 3 --hotkey OWNER_HOTKEY --remove-cap
```

To have a cap from the first credit, open the deposit before the first emission
arrives. Sign with the owner coldkey and mint a small amount to the owner hotkey
on the target subnet, with the cap that you want. Later credits keep that cap.

```bash
btcli gamma mint --netuid 3 --hotkey OWNER_HOTKEY --amount-tao 1 --daily-spend-cap-gamma 0
```

The cap belongs to the deposit, not to the hotkey. The chain removes a deposit
when its coldkey closes it, moves, withdraws or sends its whole balance, or
swaps its hotkey while it holds gamma. If gamma emission credits the same hotkey after
that, it opens a new deposit **without a cap**. Before runtime 495 that deposit
opened paused. To keep a cap or a pause on the owner hotkey, do not close its
deposit and do not move, withdraw or send its whole balance.

Gamma emission credits the owner hotkey of the subnet that pays the share. A
hotkey swap on the target subnet only moves the deposit to the new hotkey, but
the paying subnet keeps the old owner hotkey. Later credits then open a new
deposit at the old hotkey, without a cap. To replace an owner hotkey, swap it on
the paying subnet too, or on all subnets.

Use an owner hotkey that you can protect. Without a cap, a person who controls
that hotkey can spend all the gamma in its deposit.

## Spending

The hotkey spends its deposit at a service the subnet lists with
[`services`](/docs/query/services), with [`spend-gamma`](/docs/tx/spend-gamma)
signed by the hotkey:

```bash
btcli query services --netuid 1
btcli gamma spend --netuid 1 --service-hotkey SERVICE_HOTKEY --amount-gamma 5 -H API_KEY
```

```python
api_key = bt.Wallet(name="my_coldkey", hotkey="API_KEY")
await client.execute(bt.SpendGamma(netuid=1, service_hotkey_ss58=service, amount_gamma=5), api_key)
```

A spend destroys its amount and adds it to what the hotkey has spent at the
service, [`gamma-spent`](/docs/query/gamma-spent), which the service credits to
the hotkey (see [Subnet services](/docs/guides/services#accepting-gamma)).

A spend is at least 0.1 gamma unless it empties the deposit, and within the
deposit's balance and what its daily spend cap allows now: `spendable_gamma` in
[`gamma-deposit`](/docs/query/gamma-deposit). It pays no fee, so the hotkey
needs no TAO; the chain refuses a spend that would fail before it enters a
block. A hotkey has one spend pending per subnet: a second is refused until the
first is in a block. A spend at a hotkey with no service on the subnet is
refused.

## The TAO/USD price

An oracle set by root feeds the TAO/USD price in gamma units, 10^9 per US
dollar, so 400 dollars per TAO is fed as 400000000000. The accepted price falls
to a lower feed at once and rises toward a higher one by at most 0.0066% (66
ppm) per block, about 2% per hour. A mint values TAO at the accepted price and
needs a feed at most 300 blocks (one hour) old; otherwise it fails with
[`TaoUsdPriceUnavailable`](/docs/errors/chain/TaoUsdPriceUnavailable). Setting
or removing the oracle clears the price, so no gamma can be minted until an
oracle feeds. Read it with [`tao-usd-price`](/docs/query/tao-usd-price):

```bash
btcli query tao-usd-price
```

## Limitations

- Gamma confers no claim on any party and cannot be redeemed. A subnet decides
  whether, and at what prices, it accepts its gamma.
- Dissolving or pruning a subnet destroys all of its gamma and clears what was
  spent at its services, and holders receive nothing.
- Spent gamma pays the service nothing on chain: the burned alpha's value
  accrues to the subnet's alpha holders.
- While a coldkey swap is announced, the coldkey's gamma calls are refused, but
  its deposits' hotkeys keep spending.
- A hotkey swap moves the coldkey's deposit at the old hotkey on each subnet it
  swaps to the new hotkey, as a move of the whole balance, or pauses it if the
  new hotkey holds another coldkey's deposit there. What the old hotkey spent
  stays under it. A deposit another coldkey holds at the old hotkey stays, and
  the old hotkey can still spend it; if that hotkey leaked, that coldkey moves
  the deposit or sets its daily spend cap to 0.
- A coldkey swap adds the coldkey's deposits and balances to the new coldkey's.
  It fails if the new coldkey would hold more than 64 deposits on a subnet, and
  is refused when the two coldkeys hold more than 128 together. A deposit at the
  coldkey's own address keeps that address as its key, so move it to another
  hotkey before announcing the swap.
- The oracle is trusted: its accepted price can rise at most about 61% per day,
  until root replaces or removes it.
