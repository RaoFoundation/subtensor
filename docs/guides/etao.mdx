**Etao** (runtime **v480**, with the root payout rule finalised in **v481**)
hands every subnet owner the dial that used to be
welded shut: how the subnet's alpha emission is divided. For five years every
subnet paid the same split — 18% to the owner, 41% to validators, 41% to
miners. Now a subnet that lives on its miners can pay them 62%. A subnet that
needs deep validation can push validators to the 50% cap. An owner who wants
to signal commitment can set their own cut to zero. One extrinsic, one rate
limit, five rules, and a guarantee underneath all of it: **root TAO stakers
are paid exactly what they would be on an untouched subnet, whatever the
owner picks.**

This page is the complete release note. It covers what changed and why, how
the vector and its rules work, how root stays whole, the migration, the
deprecated calls, every new storage item, event, runtime API, EVM function
and SDK/CLI surface, worked CLI examples for owners, and what validators,
miners, root stakers and integrators should expect.

## Summary

- **New:** a per-subnet **emission weight vector** `[burn, recycle, owner,
  validator, miner]` of `u64` parts of `u64::MAX` that sums to exactly one.
- **New extrinsic:** `AdminUtils.sudo_set_subnet_emission_weights(netuid,
  uids, values)` — a sparse update callable by the subnet owner (once per
  50,400 blocks per subnet) or root.
- **Rules (v1):** `burn + recycle ≤ 33%`; `0 ≤ owner ≤ 33%` (lease-created
  subnets `≥ 18%`); `validator ≤ 50%` and strictly above root's live
  baseline share; `miner ≥ 33%`; the five slots sum to exactly one.
- **Root stays whole:** every block, root TAO stakers are paid their
  **baseline share** of the subnet's emission — what they would earn with
  the default validator share and the global `TaoWeight`, recomputed from
  live chain state — **off the top of the validator slot**, capped at the
  slot. Alpha validators share what is left. Nothing the owner sets, and no
  later drift in stake or issuance, moves root's payout away from the
  baseline.
- **Migration:** every live subnet is seeded from its legacy owner-cut state.
  Payouts in the first post-upgrade block are identical to the last
  pre-upgrade block.
- **Deprecated:** `sudo_set_subnet_owner_cut` and `sudo_set_owner_cut_enabled`
  now return `Deprecated`; the owner slot replaces both.
- **Surfaces:** runtime API `get_subnet_emission_weights`, event
  `SubnetEmissionWeightsSet`, three hyperparameters (`owner_emission`,
  `validator_emission`, `miner_emission`), EVM
  `setSubnetEmissionWeights` / `getSubnetEmissionWeights`, the
  `set_subnet_emission_weights` intent and `subnet_emission_weights` read in
  the Python SDK, and `btcli subnets emission` with `show`, `set`,
  `set-value`, `increase` and `decrease`.
- **Ships** to mainnet as a sudo runtime upgrade from `main`; `spec_version`
  481 (480 introduced the vector; 481 replaced its set-time TAO-weight solve
  with the per-block root payout described here and removed the
  `SubnetTaoWeight` storage item).

## Why

Bittensor subnets are not alike. Some are miner-heavy compute markets where
validation is cheap and the scarce resource is miner effort. Some are
judgment-heavy and need validators to spend real money to score work. Some
are owner-run products where the owner cut funds the team; others want the
owner cut gone so every token flows to participants. A single fixed split
cannot serve all of them, and until now the only knob — the owner cut — was a
global sudo setting, the same for every subnet.

The obstacle to letting owners move the validator share was root. Root TAO
stakers are paid from the validator slot of every subnet in proportion to
their TAO-weighted stake. Let an owner cut the validator slot from 41% to
20% and, with nothing else changing, root's income from that subnet would
halve. Owners would be able to tax a network-wide stakeholder to pay their
own participants. Etao removes that coupling: the validator slot becomes a
choice about *validators*, and root's payout is pinned to a baseline that
the owner cannot move.

## The emission weight vector

Every subnet carries a five-slot vector that says how each block's alpha
emission is divided:

| Slot | Name | Paid to | v1 rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `burn` | nothing — alpha is destroyed (still counts toward issuance) | with recycle, `≤ 33%` combined |
| 1 | `recycle` | nothing — alpha leaves `SubnetAlphaOut` and `TotalAlphaIssuance` | with burn, `≤ 33%` combined |
| 2 | `owner` | the subnet owner's hotkey at each epoch | `0 ≤ owner ≤ 33%`; lease-created subnets `≥ 18%` |
| 3 | `validator` | root's baseline share first, then alpha validator dividends | `≤ 50%`, and strictly above root's baseline share |
| 4 | `miner` | miner incentive, split across the subnet's mechanisms | `≥ 33%` |

Each slot is a `u64` share of `u64::MAX`, so the five slots sum to exactly
one with no rounding dust. Per block, `alpha_out × burn` is burned,
`alpha_out × recycle` is recycled, `alpha_out × owner` accrues to the
owner, `alpha_out × validator` to the validator bucket and
`alpha_out × miner` to the miner bucket. Burn + recycle together may be at
most 33%.

The owner slot at zero means "no owner cut". The old `owner_cut_enabled`
reading is now simply `owner > 0`; the auto-lock of the owner cut
(`owner_cut_auto_lock_enabled`) is unchanged and applies to whatever the
owner slot pays.

### Setting it

```
AdminUtils.sudo_set_subnet_emission_weights(netuid: u16, uids: Vec<u16>, values: Vec<u64>)
```

The update is **sparse**: `uids[i]` names a slot and `values[i]` its new
share. Omitted slots keep their current value. The merged vector must pass
every rule, and each rule has its own error so a rejection names the
constraint:

| Error | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `BurnRecycleEmissionAboveMaximum` | burn + recycle > 33% |
| `OwnerEmissionAboveMaximum` | owner > 33% |
| `LeasedSubnetOwnerCutBelowMinimum` | lease-created subnet with owner < 18% |
| `ValidatorEmissionAboveMaximum` | validator > 50% |
| `ValidatorEmissionTooLowToPreserveRoot` | validator ≤ root's live baseline share, so alpha validators would get nothing (see below) |
| `MinerEmissionBelowMinimum` | miner < 33% |
| `EmissionWeightsDoNotSumToOne` | the five slots do not sum to exactly `u64::MAX` |
| `EmissionWeightsNotSettableOnRoot` | netuid 0 has no alpha emission to divide |
| `EmissionWeightIndexOutOfRange` / `EmissionWeightIndexDuplicated` / `EmissionWeightsLengthMismatch` | malformed `uids` / `values` |

Callers: the subnet owner coldkey, or root. Both obey every rule including
the lease floor, and both respect the admin freeze window. Because the slots
must still sum to one, lowering one slot means raising another in the same
call; `btcli` does that arithmetic for you (below).

### Rate limit

Owners may change a subnet's vector **once every 50,400 blocks** (about
seven days at 12 s). The limit is keyed by **subnet**, not by owner coldkey
(`LastRateLimitedBlock[OwnerHyperparamUpdate(netuid, EmissionWeights)]`), so
handing a subnet to a new owner does not reset it. A subnet's first change is
never rate-limited, root is exempt, and dissolving a subnet clears the key so
a reused netuid starts fresh. The window is a runtime constant
(`SubnetEmissionWeightsRateLimit`), not a hyperparameter.

## How root stays whole

Root TAO is weighted against alpha by the global TAO weight `w`
(`TaoWeight`, 0.18 on mainnet today, governance-set). Before Etao, each
block's alpha to root stakers from a subnet was

```
root_alpha = V × x / (x + A) × alpha_out        with x = root_tao × w
```

where `V` was the fixed 41% validator slot and `A` the subnet's alpha
issuance. Root's share of a subnet's emission was the same formula
everywhere, and it moved only with root stake, issuance and the global knob.

Etao keeps exactly that payout and detaches it from the owner's choice of
`V`:

### Root off the top

Every block, for every subnet, the chain computes root's **baseline share**

```
r = V0 × x / (x + A)        with x = root_tao × TaoWeight
```

`V0` is the validator share of the default vector (0.41 on mainnet, derived
from the default owner cut; one global constant, no per-subnet storage).
`r` is what root would earn from the subnet right now if the owner had never
touched the vector. Root is then paid

```
root_alpha = min(V, r) × alpha_out
```

off the top of the validator slot, and alpha validators share the remainder,
`(V − min(V, r)) × alpha_out`. The owner and miner slots are untouched.

Because `r` is recomputed from live state on every block, root's payout
tracks the baseline **continuously**: as alpha issuance grows against root
TAO, as root stake moves, or as governance changes `TaoWeight`, root earns
exactly what an untouched subnet would pay at that block — with no further
call from anyone. A subnet whose owner has never changed the vector pays
`V0 × x / (x + A)`, identical to the chain before Etao.

Two consequences:

- **The owner cannot move root's payout.** Lowering `V` from 41% to 20%
  takes the whole 21 points from alpha validators; root's slice is the same
  before and after. Raising `V` likewise pays only alpha validators more.
  There is no per-subnet weight to solve, store or ratchet.
- **Everything else is the plain global `TaoWeight`.** Stake weighting
  inside Yuma Consensus, the TAO-versus-alpha split of validator dividends,
  the child/parent stake inheritance and the pool side all use the global
  knob exactly as before Etao.

### When a change is rejected

The set call fails with `ValidatorEmissionTooLowToPreserveRoot` when the
requested validator share is **at or below root's live baseline**,
`V_new ≤ r`: root would take the entire slot and alpha validators would
receive nothing. The floor is therefore `r` itself, not a multiple of it.

On a mainnet subnet with 5.44M TAO on root, 6.46M alpha issued and
`TaoWeight = 0.18`, the baseline `r` is about 5.40% of emission, so the
validator slot can go down to anything strictly above **5.40%**. The floor
moves with root stake, the subnet's issuance and the global weight, so
`btcli subnets emission show` prints the live value, and every write checks
it locally before anything is signed. With no root stake there is nothing to
preserve and any positive validator share is accepted.

### The capped case

The check runs at set time; the baseline keeps moving afterwards. If a subnet
sets `V` just above today's `r` and the baseline later grows past it (root
stake rises, issuance falls, or governance raises `TaoWeight`), root's cut is
**capped at the whole validator slot**: root is paid `V × alpha_out` and alpha
validators receive nothing until the owner raises `V` again. Root is never
paid more than the slot, and the owner can always restore validators by
raising the share (root is exempt from the rate limit, so governance can fix
a stuck subnet immediately). `btcli subnets emission show` flags the capped
state and the runtime API reports both `root_baseline_share` and the
`root_share` actually paid.

### What does not change

The **pool side** is unchanged. `tao_in`, `alpha_in`, the alpha-injection
cap and `RootProp` use the global `TaoWeight`, exactly as before. Changing a
subnet's vector changes how its alpha is **distributed**; it does not change
how much TAO or alpha is **injected** into its pool, and it does not change
prices, swaps or the emission the subnet receives.

## Migration and compatibility

The v480 upgrade ran one migration, `seed_subnet_emission_weights_v1`,
which seeds every live subnet so that **the first post-upgrade block pays out
exactly what the last pre-upgrade block did**:

- `owner` = the legacy `SubnetOwnerCut` scaled to `u64` parts when
  `OwnerCutEnabled` was true, else 0;
- `validator` = half of the remainder (the old 50/50 split);
- `miner` = whatever is left, absorbing the rounding ulp;
- `SubnetCreatedViaLease` = true for subnets with a live lease mapping (the
  lease floor on the owner slot applies to them);
- `SubnetOwnerCutAccrued` = `alpha_issuance × legacy SubnetOwnerCut` — the
  exact deduction the legacy lock refund applied — so dissolving a subnet
  later pays no more than it would have. Subnets already mid-dissolution get
  this seeded too, so their pending refund is correct.

The v481 upgrade runs `remove_subnet_tao_weight_v1`, which deletes every
`SubnetTaoWeight` entry the v480 set-time solve wrote (the per-block baseline
replaces them) and touches nothing else. One side effect to know about:
subnets whose legacy owner cut was *disabled* were seeded at v480 with a 50%
validator slot and, under the v480 rule, paid root `0.50 × x / (x + A)`.
Under v481 they pay the global baseline `0.41 × x / (x + A)` like every other
subnet — about 18% less root alpha on those subnets, and correspondingly more
to their alpha validators.

Newly registered subnets are seeded with the default vector
`[0, 0, 18%, 41%, 41%]`. Dissolving a subnet clears its vector, lease
marker, accrued owner cut and rate-limit key, so a reused netuid starts
clean.

**Deprecated extrinsics.** `AdminUtils.sudo_set_subnet_owner_cut` and
`AdminUtils.sudo_set_owner_cut_enabled` keep their call indices and SCALE
signatures but always return `Error::Deprecated`. Set the owner slot
(including to zero) through `sudo_set_subnet_emission_weights` instead. The
`SubnetOwnerCut` and `OwnerCutEnabled` storage items remain readable as
legacy views but are no longer written.

## New chain surface

**Storage** (pallet `SubtensorModule`):

| Item | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SubnetEmissionWeights` | `netuid → BoundedVec<u64, 5>` | the vector; defaults to `[0, 0, 18%, 41%, 41%]` |
| `SubnetCreatedViaLease` | `netuid → bool` | marks lease-created subnets (owner floor 18%) |
| `SubnetOwnerCutAccrued` | `netuid → alpha` | owner cut paid out so far; deducted from the lock refund on dissolution |

**Event:** `SubtensorModule.SubnetEmissionWeightsSet { netuid, weights }`
carries the full post-merge five-slot vector.

**Rate limit:** `TransactionType::OwnerHyperparamUpdate(Hyperparameter::EmissionWeights)`
keyed by subnet in `LastRateLimitedBlock`; constant
`SubnetEmissionWeightsRateLimit = 50_400`.

**Runtime API / RPC:** `SubnetInfoRuntimeApi.get_subnet_emission_weights(netuid)
→ Option<SubnetEmissionWeightsInfo { netuid, weights, burn, recycle, owner,
validator, miner, root_baseline_share, root_share, created_via_lease }>` (API
version 3). `root_baseline_share` is `r` and `root_share` is `min(V, r)`, both
in `u64::MAX` parts at the queried block.
`get_subnet_hyperparams_v3` also reports `owner_emission`,
`validator_emission` and `miner_emission` as `u64` parts.

**EVM** (subnet precompile, index 2051):

```solidity
function setSubnetEmissionWeights(uint16 netuid, uint16[] calldata uids, uint64[] calldata values) external;
function getSubnetEmissionWeights(uint16 netuid) external view returns (uint64[] memory weights, bool createdViaLease);
```

Shares are `uint64 / type(uint64).max`. The setter is dispatched from the
caller's mapped coldkey and must pass the same rules; a rejected set reverts
with the chain error name.

**Python SDK:**

- read `subnet_emission_weights(netuid)` → vector, shares, the global TAO
  weight, root's baseline share and the share actually paid, what alpha
  validators keep after root, whether root's cut is capped, the live
  validator floor, lease flag, rate-limit state;
- intent `SetSubnetEmissionWeights(netuid, slots, values, renormalize=False)`
  → sparse set with an optional proportional renormalisation of the untouched
  slots; its plan lists every rule the chain would reject and a structured
  `preview` (before/after vector, root's share before and after, what alpha
  validators keep before and after, the capped flag, rate limit, allowed
  ranges);
- module `bittensor.emission_weights` with the pure rules
  (`validate`, `renormalize`, `baseline_root_share`, `validator_floor`,
  `root_share_paid`, `preview`) so you can check a vector offline.

**CLI:** `btcli subnets emission show | set | set-value | increase | decrease`.

a vector the chain's rules would reject.


before anything reaches the chain.

## Notes by role

**Validators.** Your dividends come from the validator slot after root's
baseline share is taken off the top. On a subnet whose owner lowers that
slot, every point removed comes out of what alpha validators and their alpha
stakers receive; root's slice does not move. The TAO-versus-alpha split
inside your dividends uses the global `TaoWeight` exactly as before. The
floor only guarantees the slot is above root's baseline at the moment it is
set; if the baseline later grows past the slot, alpha validators receive
nothing until the owner raises it (`show` reports this as capped). Watch
`SubnetEmissionWeightsSet` events or `btcli subnets emission show` on the
subnets you validate.

**Miners.** The miner slot is at least 33% and can grow to whatever is left
after the owner slot and the validator floor — with the owner cut at zero
and the validator slot at its floor, most of the subnet's emission. Nothing
about weight setting, incentive or the epoch changes; only the size of the
bucket.

**Root TAO stakers.** Your share of every subnet's emission is its
baseline — what the subnet would pay you with the default validator share
and the global `TaoWeight` — recomputed every block from live root stake,
issuance and the global weight, and paid before alpha validators see
anything. Owners cannot move it in either direction, and it needs no call
from anyone to stay current. The only cap is the validator slot itself: on a
subnet whose slot has fallen below the baseline you receive the whole slot.
The global `TaoWeight` remains the governance lever over root's weight.

**Subnet owners.** You get one change per week per subnet; plan it. Your
first change is free of the limit. Lease-created subnets cannot drop the
owner slot below 18%. Every `btcli` write validates locally and shows the
exact floor, so test with `--dry-run` first. The owner cut's auto-lock
setting is unaffected.

**Integrators and explorers.** Read the vector with the
`get_subnet_emission_weights` runtime API or the `subnet_emission_weights`
SDK read; the three hyperparameters are also in `get_subnet_hyperparams_v3`.
Treat `SubnetOwnerCut` / `OwnerCutEnabled` as legacy views. There is no
per-subnet TAO weight: consensus and pool side both use the global
`TaoWeight`, and root's per-subnet payout is `min(V, r) × alpha_out` with
`r` from the formula above (the runtime API returns both numbers). Expect
`SubnetEmissionWeightsSet` events; the two deprecated extrinsics now fail
with `Deprecated`.
