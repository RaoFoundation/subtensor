Quotes and makes [gamma](/docs/guides/gamma) mints that credit another
coldkey's deposit, and reads deposits. Gamma amounts are in base units, 10^9 per
gamma; TAO amounts in rao.

## Functions

```text
quoteMintGamma(uint256,bytes32,bytes32,uint256)
quoteMintGammaWithStake(uint256,bytes32,bytes32,bytes32,uint256)
getGammaDeposit(uint256,bytes32)
mintGammaOnBehalf(uint256,bytes32,bytes32,uint256,uint256)
mintGammaWithStakeOnBehalf(uint256,bytes32,bytes32,bytes32,uint256,uint256)
```

The quotes fail on any error that stops the mint, including the checks on the
deposit they would credit. `quoteMintGammaWithStake` reads no stake, so it
quotes the whole amount. The mints are not payable: they dispatch as the mapped
EVM caller, which pays the TAO from its free balance or burns its own stake, and
return the gamma added to the deposit.
