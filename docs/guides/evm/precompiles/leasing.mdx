## Operations

```text
createLeaseCrowdloan(uint64,uint64,uint64,uint32,uint8,bool,uint32)
terminateLease(uint32,bytes32)
```

Both operations are `payable`.

Before handing over the residual stake, final settlement allocates every
contributor's share of accumulated dividends to that contributor. Funded final
payments include dust below the ordinary stake-transfer minimum; they are not
written off or reassigned to the beneficiary.

The first successful `terminateLease` hands ownership to the beneficiary and
removes the beneficiary proxy. If genuine unpaid debts remain, the lease record
stays available for recovery. Later calls and the ended-lease hook settle those
debts without taking ownership away from a conviction winner or removing the
proxy again. Old partially terminated records are recognized as well.

Once contributors are fully settled, the synthetic account's remaining stake
and its lock follow the beneficiary, including dust whose current TAO valuation
rounds to zero. Lock aggregates follow the same owner/hotkey transition rules
as conviction-based ownership changes. Ordinary user transfers retain their
minimum. Unfunded debts or real lock conflicts still preserve the debt and its
backing for a later retry.
