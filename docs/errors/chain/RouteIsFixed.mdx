The bridge exists, and its route (chain, transport domain, portal and stablecoin) cannot change: what the bridge has outstanding is in the vault of its portal. `Tusd.set_bridge` changes only the mint cap and the mint window of an existing bridge. A new portal is a new bridge, with a new id.

Declared by the `Tusd` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`policy_violation`](/docs/errors/policy-violation).
