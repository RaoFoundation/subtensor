The short's collateral and sale proceeds together are below its coverage need, on an open or increase, or for the part that a partial close leaves. Read the least collateral from `LendingRuntimeApi.quote_open_short` or `quote_increase_short` (`min_collateral`) and bring at least that much, or reduce `q`.

Declared by the `Lending` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`insufficient_balance`](/docs/errors/insufficient-balance).

Declared at [`pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L490`](/code/pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L490).

## Remediation

Fund the signing account or reduce the amount; check with `btcli wallet balance`

The same explanation is available in the terminal: `btcli explain CoverageNotMet`.
