Collateral plus sale proceeds miss the position's share of buying the whole subnet book back after a stressed pump, or the pumped pool cannot supply the book at all. Check lending_open_quote for the required collateral, add collateral, or mint less.

Declared by the `Lending` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`insufficient_balance`](/docs/errors/insufficient-balance).

Declared at [`pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L835`](/code/pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L835).

## Remediation

Fund the signing account or reduce the amount; check with `btcli wallet balance`
