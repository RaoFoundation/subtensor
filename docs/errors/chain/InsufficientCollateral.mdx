The requested additional debt needs more collateral under V_dyn / LTV checks. Use lending_required_collateral (or btcli loan open --amount) for the minimum size, then pass at least that much --collateral, or lower --amount. The failed call moves no assets.

Declared by the `Lending` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`insufficient_balance`](/docs/errors/insufficient-balance).

Declared at [`pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L1313`](/code/pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L1313).

## Remediation

Fund the signing account or reduce the amount; check with `btcli wallet balance`
