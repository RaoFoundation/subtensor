The subnet is being dissolved, so no short on it can be opened, increased, closed or given collateral: from the moment the subnet is flagged until its cleanup ends, the dissolution alone settles its shorts. An open short is settled at the dissolution rate and what is left of its collateral, proceeds and deposit is returned to the coldkey; no call is needed. `Lending.DissolvingSubnets` has an entry for the subnet while this lasts.

Declared by the `Lending` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`policy_violation`](/docs/errors/policy-violation).
