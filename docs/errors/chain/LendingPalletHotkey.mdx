The hotkey is the lending pallet's own hotkey, which holds the alpha of lending custody accounts. Nobody else may stake or lock to it, register it, swap to it, name it as a child or parent, or use it as a subnet owner hotkey. Use a different hotkey.

Declared by the `SubtensorModule` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`not_authorized`](/docs/errors/not-authorized).

Declared at [`pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L519`](/code/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L519).
