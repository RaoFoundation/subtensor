Closing the long would not repay its debt: the TAO from selling its alpha plus its collateral is less than the TAO it lifted from the pool. Add collateral with `add_long_collateral` before closing.

Declared by the `Lending` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`insufficient_balance`](/docs/errors/insufficient-balance).

Declared at [`pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L502`](/code/pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L502).

## Remediation

Fund the signing account or reduce the amount; check with `btcli wallet balance`

The same explanation is available in the terminal: `btcli explain CloseInsolvent`.
