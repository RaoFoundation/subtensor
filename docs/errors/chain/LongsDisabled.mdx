Longs are switched off (`Derivatives.LongsEnabled` is false, shown as `longs_enabled` in `deriv params`): shorts are the launch product, and longs wait on a decision about their collateral and leverage. No add may open a long, grow one, or flip a short through zero into one. Shorts work as usual with `deriv short`; a `deriv long` that only reduces or closes a short goes through; an open long can still be closed with `deriv close`. Governance turns longs on with `Derivatives.sudo_set_longs_enabled`.

Declared by the `Derivatives` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`disabled`](/docs/errors/disabled).

Declared at [`pallets/derivatives/src/lib.rs#L341`](/code/pallets/derivatives/src/lib.rs#L341).

## Remediation

Longs are not enabled at launch (`longs_enabled` in `btcli deriv params` is false); open or add to a short with `btcli deriv short` instead, or close an open position with `btcli deriv close`

The same explanation is available in the terminal: `btcli explain LongsDisabled`.
