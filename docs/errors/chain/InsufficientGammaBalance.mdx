The spend or transfer is above the gamma deposit's balance, or the transfer is above the coldkey's gamma balance. The `gamma_deposit` read gives `spendable_gamma`, what the hotkey can spend now, and `gamma_balances` a coldkey's balances.

Declared by the `SubtensorModule` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`insufficient_balance`](/docs/errors/insufficient-balance).

Declared at [`pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L489`](/code/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L489).

## Remediation

Fund the signing account or reduce the amount; check with `btcli wallet balance`

The same explanation is available in the terminal: `btcli explain InsufficientGammaBalance`.
