A root claim would process more than its admission envelope. A coldkey-wide claim admits 256 units: only root-relevant validator hotkeys and their stored basket rows count as claim work; classifying the staking-hotkey relationship vector is separately capped at 256, and the queued dividend credits the claim would flush first are capped at 512 across all validators claimed. Unrelated subnet stakes are not multiplied by the total network count. A single-validator claim admits 258 units: the validator, its stored basket rows, and one for each queued dividend credit on a subnet the fund does not hold. A runtime before 495 admits 130 units and does not count queued credits. A single-validator claim also fails with this error when a `swap_basket` buy or a hotkey swap gave the same fund a new row earlier in the same transaction; send the claim in a later transaction. Split a coldkey-wide claim by validator where that fits, and investigate or consolidate an individually oversized basket.

Declared by the `SubtensorModule` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`limit_exceeded`](/docs/errors/limit-exceeded).
