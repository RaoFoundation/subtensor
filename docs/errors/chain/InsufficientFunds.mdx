The short's proceeds and collateral together cannot pay for buying back the alpha being closed. Compare `cost` from `LendingRuntimeApi.quote_close_short` with the position's `collateral` and proceeds in `Lending.Positions`; add collateral with `add_collateral` or close a smaller amount.

Declared by the `Lending` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`insufficient_balance`](/docs/errors/insufficient-balance).

Declared at [`pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L500`](/code/pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L500).

## Remediation

Fund the signing account or reduce the amount; check with `btcli wallet balance`

The same explanation is available in the terminal: `btcli explain InsufficientFunds`.
