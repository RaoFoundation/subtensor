Locked alpha was being transferred to a coldkey whose `AccountFlags` do not have the accept-locked-alpha bit set, e.g. during a stake transfer that moves conviction lock mass or a coldkey swap of locks. Check the destination coldkey's `AccountFlags` storage and have the recipient opt in with `SubtensorModule.set_reject_locked_alpha(false)` before retrying. See [Transferring locked stake](/docs/guides/conviction#transferring-locked-stake).

Declared by the `SubtensorModule` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`policy_violation`](/docs/errors/policy-violation).
