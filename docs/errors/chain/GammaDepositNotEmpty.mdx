Only an empty gamma deposit can be closed. Moving its whole balance with `move_gamma` closes it; once its spender has spent it to zero, close it.

Declared by the `SubtensorModule` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`invalid_argument`](/docs/errors/invalid-argument).

Declared at [`pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L475`](/code/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L475).

## Remediation

Move the balance out with `btcli deposits move --amount-gamma all`, which closes the deposit, or close it once its spender has spent it
