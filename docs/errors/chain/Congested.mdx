The bridge is congested: `Rails.CongestedAt` or more of its messages are deferred, because the transport charges more for them than the hub pays and nobody paid the difference. Until some of them have left, the hub makes no new payment on that chain: it refunds an order, and refuses an exit. Anyone can send a deferred message and pay the difference (`btcli bridge flush --message`), or pay into the bridge's account (`Rails.fund_messages`). `btcli bridge list` shows the deferred messages.
