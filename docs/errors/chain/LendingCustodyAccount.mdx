The coldkey of a stake, lock or key-swap call is an account of the lending pallet (a position's custody account, the pallet account or the pallet hotkey). Those accounts hold collateral and locked alpha for open positions and are moved only by lending itself. Use the position holder's own coldkey and the lending calls instead.

Declared by the `SubtensorModule` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`not_authorized`](/docs/errors/not-authorized).

Declared at [`pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L514`](/code/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs#L514).

## Remediation

Sign with the key or origin that owns the target object, then retry
