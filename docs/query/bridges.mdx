`outstanding_tusd` is the tUSD the bridge minted and has not burned: the
stablecoin its vault must hold. `mint_cap_tusd` bounds it. `tao_supply`
and `tao_cap` are the canonical TAO on the bridge's chain and its cap.

The last eight fields say who pays for the hub's messages to the bridge's
chain. `message_account` holds the TAO for them, and `message_budget` is
what it can pay: its balance less the existential deposit. `message_reserve`
is what the hub sets aside for each message, or None while it is not set,
and `message_ceiling` is the most the hub pays for one: above the reserve
it pays from what the account can spare. `messages_waiting` is how many
have not left the hub. `messages_deferred` lists the numbers of those
among them that are deferred: they left the line because the transport
charged more for them than was paid, or the gateway could not hand them
over, and the gateway sends each one when somebody asks it to (`btcli
bridge flush --message`).

`order_slots` and `max_order_slots` say how many orders the bridge's chain
may send. A slot is the hub's promise to answer one order: the hub keeps a
place in the bridge's queue and a reserve in the account for that answer.
The portal on the other chain takes an order only if it holds a slot.
`order_slots` counts the slots that are out: at the portal, on their way
to it, or used by an order that the hub has not taken yet. The hub gives
slots back with its payments, up to `max_order_slots`, as far as the
account covers them. Anyone can pay into the account
(`Rails.fund_messages`).
