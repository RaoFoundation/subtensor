Burns `shares` (`all` for every share you hold) and pays their part of
both reserves, which includes the swap fees the pool earned since you
added. Open even while the pool is disabled. Fails rather than receiving
less of either asset than the reserves now give, less `rate_tolerance`.

| Signer | Origin | Pallet | Wraps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `coldkey` | signed account (pallet role may apply) | CanonicalPool | [`CanonicalPool.remove_liquidity`](/code/pallets/canonical-pool/src/lib.rs#L347-L357) |

## Parameters

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `shares` | string | yes | Shares to redeem, a whole number, or all. |
| `rate_tolerance` | number | no | Maximum fall of what you receive below the current quote, as a fraction (0.05 = 5%). |

Address parameters (`--hotkey`, `--coldkey`, `--dest`, ...) accept a raw ss58
address, an address-book or proxy-book name, or a local wallet/hotkey name.
