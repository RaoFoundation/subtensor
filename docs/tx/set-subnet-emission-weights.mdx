Dispatches `AdminUtils.sudo_set_subnet_emission_weights` for the named
slots. The signer must be the subnet's owner coldkey (root may also call
it). With `renormalize` the untouched owner/validator/miner slots are
rescaled so the vector still sums to one; without it the merged vector is
submitted as given. Every accepted set makes the chain re-solve the
subnet's effective TAO weight so root stakers earn their baseline share of
emission (what they would earn with the default validator share and the
global TaoWeight) — the pre-sign plan shows that share and the resulting
weight, and lists every rule the chain would reject (including a validator
share too low to reach the baseline), so a bad vector is caught before
signing. Owners may change the vector once per week per subnet.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `netuid` | integer | yes | Subnet to configure; the signer must be its owner. |
| `slots` | array of ['integer', 'string'] | yes | Slots to set, by name or uid: 0 burn, 1 recycle, 2 owner, 3 validator, 4 miner. Omitted owner/validator/miner slots keep their current share; omitted burn/recycle are zero. |
| `values` | array of ['integer', 'number', 'string'] | yes | New share for each slot, pairwise with `slots`: a 0..1 fraction (0.41), a percentage ("41%"), or raw u64 parts of 2^64-1. Rules: burn = recycle = 0; owner \<= 33% (>= 18% on lease-created subnets); validator \<= 50% and high enough that root's baseline share of emission is reachable (see `btcli subnets emission show`); miner >= 33%; all five sum to exactly 1. |
| `renormalize` | boolean | no | Keep the named slots as given and rescale the other owner/validator/miner slots proportionally so the vector sums to 1 (the miner slot absorbs rounding). Without it, the update is applied verbatim and must already sum to 1. |

Address parameters (`--hotkey`, `--coldkey`, `--dest`, ...) accept a raw ss58
address, an address-book or proxy-book name, or a local wallet/hotkey name.
