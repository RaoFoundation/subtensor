---
title: owner_cut_enabled
description: Derived flag — whether the subnet owner takes any share of alpha emission (true iff the owner emission slot is non-zero).
---

Read-only since the per-subnet [emission weight
vector](/docs/concepts/emission-weights) replaced the global owner cut. The
flag is `true` exactly when the subnet's
[`owner_emission`](/docs/hyperparameters/owner-emission) slot is greater than
zero. Owners care because the cut is their revenue; stakers and miners care
because whatever the owner does not take is redistributed to them.

## How it works

Every block, the chain accrues `alpha_out × owner_emission` to a pending
owner bucket; at the next epoch that alpha is staked directly to the subnet
owner's hotkey and coldkey, while the validator and miner slots go through
Yuma Consensus (see [the per-tempo
split](/docs/concepts/emissions#the-per-tempo-split)). With the owner slot at
zero no deduction happens: the full `alpha_out` flows to participants and the
owner receives nothing — the forgone cut is not stashed or paid
retroactively. Blocks are settled as they accrue, so changing the slot
mid-tempo affects only the blocks after the change.

Subnets that never set a vector keep the historical 18% default
(`11796/65535`). Lease-created subnets may not drop the owner slot below
that default, so on those subnets this flag cannot become `false`.

## Reading and setting

```bash
btcli sudo get --netuid 12 --name owner_cut_enabled
btcli subnets emission set-value --netuid 12 --slot owner --value 0      # disable
btcli subnets emission set-value --netuid 12 --slot owner --value 0.18   # restore
```

The former `sudo_set_owner_cut_enabled` and `sudo_set_subnet_owner_cut`
calls are deprecated and always fail with `Deprecated`; set the owner slot
through [`set-subnet-emission-weights`](/docs/tx/set-subnet-emission-weights)
instead (subnet owner, once per week per subnet, or root). Not permitted on
the root subnet.
