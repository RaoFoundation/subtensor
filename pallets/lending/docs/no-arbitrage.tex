\begin{abstract}
Following $\mu$'s method: for each non-trivial action cycle that
\textbf{starts and ends holding only TAO}, write the terminal TAO as a
factor of the initial holding, sized as a fraction $h$ of pool TAO, as a
function of LP state $(y,x,w_1)$ and lending parameters. Then minimise /
maximise that factor over the parameter space and ask whether any region
has expected value $>1$ (attacker profit).

This increment records: (i)~state and action set; (ii)~$\mu$'s September
$F$ formula (transcription checked against the hand derivation);
(iii)~a provisional map of $(f_h,\varphi,w_2)$ into the current lift
design; (iv)~the first TAO-pure cycles under forced-escrow longs/shorts;
(v)~numeric $F$ on SN29-like reserves. Later increments add remaining
cycles, closed-form extrema, the weight-ratchet counterexample, and a
bounded search that mirrors pallet math.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method (from $\mu$)}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Enumerate every non-trivial finite sequence of stake/unstake swaps
        and lending calls that \textbf{begins and ends with a pure-TAO
        book} (no open position, no residual alpha inventory attributed
        to the cycle).
  \item Write terminal TAO $V'$ as
        \[
          V' = F_{\mathrm{cyc}}(h;\, y,x,w_1;\, \Theta)\, V,
        \]
        where $V$ is the initial TAO stake of the cycle, $h = V/y$ (or
        another natural scale against pool TAO $y$), and $\Theta$ collects
        lending parameters (LTV, thin LTV, swap fee, EMA band, APR, term,
        capacity floors).
  \item Over a compact parameter domain (including SN29-like and deep
        equal-weight pools), compute
        $\min F_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ and $\max F_{\mathrm{cyc}}$.
  \item Positive EV for that cycle $\iff$ some feasible point has
        $F_{\mathrm{cyc}} > 1$ after fees (attacker convention below).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{remark}[Sign convention]
We take $F_{\mathrm{cyc}} > 1$ as \textbf{attacker profit} (ends with more
TAO than started, marking-to-market any returned alpha at the exit swap).
$\mu$'s September note used ``profitable when $F<1$'': that $F$ is a
\textbf{cost / survival factor for the pool (or for the round-trip
price)}, i.e.\ the reciprocal convention. We keep $\mu$'s symbol $F$ for
the September formula unchanged, and write $F_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ for
attacker-return factors. Relation when both apply to the same cycle:
$F_{\mathrm{cyc}} = 1/F$ up to fee placement.
\end{remark}

\section{State and parameters}
\label{sec:state}

\subsection{LP / Balancer state}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Reserves: TAO $y$, alpha $x$.
  \item Weights: $w_1$ (base / alpha), $w_2 = 1-w_1$ (quote / TAO).
        Spot $p = (w_1/w_2)\,(y/x)$.
  \item Invariant (Balancer): $L = x^{w_1} y^{w_2}$. At $w_1=w_2=\tfrac12$,
        $L=\sqrt{xy}$ (``$\sqrt{k}$'').
  \item Swap fee: protocol default $\phi_{\mathrm{swap}} = 33/65535
        \approx 5.03\cdot 10^{-4}$. In $\mu$'s formula the fee symbol is
        $\varphi$; see §\ref{sec:mu-map}.
\end{itemize}

Lift book $(L_S,L_L,E_S,B_L,\ldots)$, positions keyed by
$(\mathrm{coldkey},\mathrm{netuid})$, EMA reference for the open band,
$G_{\mathrm{ema}}$ for capacity. One-year term and 100\% APR burn-all
interest are part of $\Theta$ but are \textbf{idle} inside a same-block
or few-block cycle (interest $\approx 0$).

\section{$\mu$'s September $F$ (earlier proposal)}
\label{sec:mu-F}

Transcription checked against the hand-derived image:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mu-F}
F
=
(f_h+1)
\left(
\frac{(1-\varphi)^{1/w_2}}
{(1-\varphi)^{1/w_2}+f_h-\varphi}
\right)^{E},
\end{equation}
with exponent
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mu-E}
E
=
\frac{f_h+1}
{(f_h+1+\varphi)
\left(
1-\dfrac{1-(1-\varphi)^{1/w_2}}{f_h-\varphi+1}
\right)^{w_2/(1-w_2)}
}.
\end{equation}

\begin{conjecture}[$\mu$, earlier design]
Along the September shorting cycle, the pool (or round-trip) factor is
$F$ above, and the cycle is attacker-profitable precisely when $F<1$.
\end{conjecture}

\subsection{Provisional variable map into current lift math}
\label{sec:mu-map}

The September proposal predated forced-escrow longs and pull-on-borrow
lifts. A working identification used in this note (to be stress-tested
against $\mu$'s full derivation when available):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & September reading & Current lift reading \\
\midrule
$w_2$ & quote weight & Balancer quote weight $w_q$ (TAO leg) \\
$\varphi$ & swap fee taken on the trade & $\phi_{\mathrm{swap}}$ (same order) \\
$f_h$ & haircut / size as fraction of pool TAO
      & candidate: $h = P/y$ after lift, or LTV-scaled
        $h=\lambda\cdot C/y$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{remark}
If $f_h$ is the \textbf{principal as a fraction of post-lift (or pre-trade)
pool TAO}, then for a capacity-bound open $h\lesssim 0.075$--$0.165$ on a
side (thin vs floor $\times$ split). For LTV-bound opens with collateral
$C=\alpha y$, $P=\lambda C$ gives $f_h=\lambda\alpha$.
\end{remark}

\section{Does the same cycle exist today?}
\label{sec:applies}

\begin{cycle}[September-style short round trip]
Open a forced short of principal $P$ alpha (lift, sell $P$ into the pool,
escrow $E_S$), then close by buying $P$ back (escrow path) or by
repaying $P$ alpha from wallet and taking $E_S$.
\end{cycle}

Under the \textbf{current} design:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sell-path / escrow close} reverses the forced sale on the
        \emph{lifted} pool then re-adds $(\tau_L,P)$ with a weight update
        that is nearly proportional. This is the intended fee-lossy
        round trip; $F_{\mathrm{cyc}}<1$ (attacker) for $\phi>0$.
  \item \textbf{Wallet-path close} returns $E_S+C$ for alpha repaid from
        outside, then re-adds $(\tau_L,P)$. If that re-add
        \emph{updates Balancer weights to preserve the post-sale spot},
        the September fee analysis is incomplete: an extra
        \textbf{weight-ratchet} term appears (see §\ref{sec:gap}).
\end{itemize}

So the September cycle \emph{still exists}, but the current
implementation adds a weight-update branch that $\mu$'s fee-only $F$ does
not capture. That is the first place a proof from $F\ge 1$ alone fails.

\section{TAO-pure cycles under the current design}
\label{sec:cycles}

Notation: attacker starts with TAO budget $V=h\,y$ (and, where needed,
an alpha inventory acquired inside the cycle by a swap that is part of
the cycle accounting).

\subsection{Cycle A --- long wallet ratchet (primary drain candidate)}
\label{cyc:A}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item (Optional) Sell alpha inventory into the pool (dump), moving spot
        down while the lending EMA lags.
  \item Open long: post collateral $C$ alpha, lift $g=P/y$, forced-buy
        escrow $A$ with $P$ TAO, subject to LTV, capacity, EMA band
        $p'\le \mathrm{EMA}\,(1+\beta)$.
  \item Close with $\mathtt{repay\_from\_wallet}=\mathsf{true}$: pay $P$
        TAO, receive $A+C$, re-add $(P,\alpha_L)$.
  \item Sell returned escrow $A$ for TAO.
\end{enumerate}
Mark-to-market: all alpha legs netted; terminal asset is TAO.
We write $F_A(h;y,x,w_1;\Theta)$ for $V'/V$.

\paragraph{Closed form (fee-free sketch, equal weights).}
Let $h=P/y\in(0,1)$, $w_1=w_2=\tfrac12$, ignore swap fees and EMA.
Lift removes $(P, hx)$ from $(y,x)$ in the sense $\alpha_L = h x$ when
$g=P/y=h$. Forced buy of $P$ on the lifted pool yields
\[
  A = (1-h)x\Bigl(1-\sqrt{\frac{1-h}{1-h+h}}\Bigr)
    = (1-h)x\Bigl(1-\sqrt{1-h}\Bigr).
\]
Post-open reserves $(y,\,x-\alpha_L-A)=(y,\,x(1-h)\sqrt{1-h})$.
Wallet repay + weight update $\to$ reserves $(y+P,\,x-A)$ with spot locked
at the post-open price; selling $A$ then gives
\[
  F_A^{\mathrm{update}}(h)
  =
  \frac{\Delta y_{\mathrm{sell}}(A;\,y+P,\,x-A,\,w')}{P},
\]
where $w'$ is the reweighted pair. With \emph{fixed} weights,
\[
  F_A^{\mathrm{keep}}(h)
  =
  \frac{y+P}{P}
  \left(
  1-\left(\frac{x-A}{x-A+A}\right)^{w_1/w_2}
  \right)
  =
  \frac{1+h}{h}
  \left(1-\left(1-\frac{A}{x}\right)\right)
  \quad\text{(for $w_1=w_2$)}
\]
reduces to a fee-free identity $F_A^{\mathrm{keep}}<1$ once the forced-buy
$A$ is substituted (exact algebra in increment~2). Numerics already show
the sign split (§\ref{sec:numeric}).

\subsection{Cycle B --- short wallet mirror}
Open short to the EMA band floor, wallet-repay with owned alpha, rebuy
alpha (or equivalently value the free $E_S$ against replacing $P$).
Factor $F_B$.

\subsection{Cycle C --- raise then wallet-close}
$\mathtt{open\_long\_and\_sell}$ with $S=A$ (near price-neutral by
design), then wallet-close the long and sell residual. Factor $F_C$.

\subsection{Cycle D --- escrow closes only}
Open long or short, close on the escrow AMM path (no wallet repay).
Fee-lossy by construction if re-add is the inverse of lift+trade.
Factors $F_D^{\mathrm{L}}, F_D^{\mathrm{S}}$.

\subsection{Cycle E --- partial repay interleaved with swaps}
Slice $k\in(0,1]$, swap, repay remainder. Factor $F_E$.

\section{Where a fee-only proof fails}
\label{sec:gap}

\begin{proposition}[Weight update on asymmetric re-add]
After a forced long buy the pool is at $(y,\,x-\alpha_L-A)$ with
\textbf{unchanged} weights and elevated spot. Wallet repay credits
$(P,\alpha_L)$ yielding reserves $(y+P,\,x-A)$. Calling
$\mathtt{update\_weights\_for\_added\_liquidity}$ sets new weights so
that the \emph{elevated} spot is preserved. Then $L=x^{w_1}y^{w_2}$
(and $w_1$) move monotonically with repeated cycles --- matching the
observed ``$w_1$ and $\sqrt{k}$ taking off''.
\end{proposition}

Consequently $\mu$'s $F$ (fees only, fixed weights) is a
\textbf{lower bound on pool protection} only for cycles that never hit
the weight-updating wallet re-add. For Cycle~A with weight update,
\[
  F_A \;\;\text{can exceed } 1
\]
even when the September $F$ satisfies $F\ge 1$.

\begin{remark}[Fix direction, not yet the proof]
Re-adding wallet-repay legs \emph{without} updating weights restores
the market-buy residual $(+P,-A)$ under fixed $(w_1,w_2)$. Numerics
(§\ref{sec:numeric}) show that change flips Cycle~A from $F_A>1$ to
$F_A<1$ on SN29-like pools. A full proof that $F_A\le 1$ under
preserve-weights is deferred to a later increment.
\end{remark}

\section{Theorem status (increment 1)}
\label{sec:status}

\begin{conjecture}[No TAO-pure arb, preserve-weights wallet re-add]
\label{conj:main}
If wallet-repay re-adds lift legs without updating Balancer weights,
and escrow closes re-add after reversing the forced trade, then for every
TAO-pure cycle of length $\le N$ (fees on, EMA fixed or free within
band), $F_{\mathrm{cyc}} \le 1$, with equality only in the zero-fee,
zero-size limit.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{theorem}[Status]
\label{thm:status}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Under \textbf{current} wallet re-add (weight-updating),
        Conjecture~\ref{conj:main} is \textbf{false}: Cycle~A is a
        counterexample with $F_A>1$ and unbounded $w_1$/$L$ under
        repetition while the EMA lags (SN29-like numbers in
        §\ref{sec:numeric}).
  \item $\mu$'s fee-only $F\ge 1$ does \textbf{not} imply
        $F_{\mathrm{cyc}}\le 1$ for Cycle~A, because $F$ omits the
        weight ratchet.
  \item Proof of Conjecture~\ref{conj:main} under preserve-weights:
        \textbf{open} (this note).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\section{Do existing protections keep $EV\le 0$?}
\label{sec:protections}

Reachable $h$ on a \textbf{single} open (equal weights):
\begin{itemize}
  \item EMA band $+15\%$: fee-free buy move
        $p'/p=(1+h)^{1/w_1}=(1+h)^2\le 1.15$ $\Rightarrow$
        $h\le \sqrt{1.15}-1 \approx 0.0724$.
  \item Side capacity: $\approx 0.075$ (thin) to $\approx 0.165$
        (floor $\times$ split).
  \item $\mathtt{PrincipalExceedsTaoIn}$ / $\mathtt{AlphaIn}$:
        $P<y$ (and symmetrically for shorts) --- far looser than the
        band/capacity.
\end{itemize}

At the EMA cap ($h\approx 0.072$) with weight-update Cycle~A:
$F_A-1 \approx {+}0.43\%$ --- \textbf{still positive EV}. At capacity
$h=0.165$ (only reachable if spot was depressed or the EMA lagged):
$\approx{+}2.7\%$. Preserve-weights is negative at both points.

\paragraph{Splits and multiple coldkeys.}
Same-side opens are gated by the \emph{sticky} EMA: after one long, spot
is up and further longs hit the band sooner. That slows naive splitting,
but does not zero EV:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $N$ sequential opens at $h=0.05$ (inside the band) each earn
        $\approx{+}0.15\%$ while $w_1$ ratchets ($0.500\to 0.513$ by
        $N=10$).
  \item A pre-dump that pushes spot below the EMA, then one long up to
        $\mathrm{EMA}\cdot 1.15$, realises a \emph{much} larger effective
        $h$ and the $10^2$--$10^3\,\tau$ profits of §\ref{sec:numeric}.
  \item Multiple coldkeys in one block still serialize; they share the
        same post-trade spot/EMA constraint, but can alternate sides or
        wait for EMA catch-up.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Protections vs Cycle~A]
\label{thm:prot}
EMA band, side capacity, and principal-vs-reserve checks \textbf{bound}
$h$ but \textbf{do not} imply $F_A\le 1$ on the weight-updating wallet
path. Inside the reachable set, $F_A>1$ for all $h\gtrsim 0.031$. Only
weight-preserving wallet re-add flips the sign through the whole
sweep.
\end{theorem}
